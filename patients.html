Aftercare

Post-operative instructions

After a filling

Wait until numbness wears off before eating. Mild sensitivity for a few days is normal. If the bite feels off and both sides of your teeth are not meeting at the same time, you may need to come in for an adjustment.

After an extraction

Bite on gauze for 30–45 minutes. Avoid straws, smoking and spitting for 24 hours.

After a crown or bridge

Avoid chewing on the temporary crown until you are able to have the permanent crown placed. Call us if it comes loose or breaks.

After root canal therapy

Some tenderness is normal for a few days. Take medication as directed.

After whitening

Avoid coffee, tea and red wine for 48 hours to protect your results.

Concerned about pain, bleeding or swelling after treatment? Call your office right away.
